Step away from the noise and reconnect

Reconneckshons Wellness Center is a sanctuary in Hampton District, St. James, Jamaica. Surrounded by the lush hills of western Jamaica, it is a retreat where the rustle of trees and the song of birds accompany your journey toward healing and inner peace.

In a world that rarely pauses, Reconneckshons invites you to exhale: to leave the noise of urban life and reconnect with the rhythms of nature, spirit and self.

Venise Samuels

Owner, CEO and founder. Intuitive healer.

Venise is a seasoned wellness guide and the visionary behind Reconneckshons. A dedicated student of naturopathy and lifelong practitioner of intuitive healing, she blends ancient wisdom with modern wellness to help people reconnect with their bodies, minds and spiritual essence.

Her approach is personal and rooted in compassion, nature and the belief that true healing begins from within.

With decades of experience and a deep commitment to holistic living, Venise welcomes visitors from around the world who are looking for authentic, Caribbean-rooted healing.

Our story

Reconneckshons was born from a vision of healing and reconnection: a haven for people seeking rest, renewal and spiritual alignment. Hampton District, with its cool breezes, gentle hills and views across the countryside, was the perfect home for that dream.

Far from the chaos of the city, yet within reach of Montego Bay, the center feels like a secret garden: untouched, unhurried and deeply nourishing. It's a place where time slows down and the soul can breathe.

Our name says what we're for: helping people reconnect with themselves, with Source, and with the natural world that sustains us. We offer a blend of traditional and modern wellness practices to guide the way.

Our mission

To help people reconnect to Source through the six R's of wellness. Each is a stepping stone toward wholeness, supported by our services and the healing energy of our natural surroundings.

  • Rest
  • Relax
  • Restore
  • Revitalize
  • Rejuvenate
  • Reconnect

Our vision

To promote, apply and sustain a holistic approach to wellness, touching lives one person, one family and one community at a time. We imagine a world where inner peace is the foundation for outer change, and we believe that change starts here in the hills of Hampton.

Why Hampton?

Hampton District is one of Jamaica's hidden gems: a rural paradise of greenery, fresh air and gentle quiet. It's the kind of place where you can hear your thoughts again, where stars still shine bright at night, and where healing happens almost effortlessly just by being present.

Our location is intentional. We wanted guests to truly step away from the hustle and reconnect with what matters. Whether you come for a weekend retreat, a mid-week yoga session or simply to be still, Hampton is the right setting for nature-assisted transformation.

Core values

Nature as healer
We believe the Earth is medicine. Our programs are rooted in the power of natural surroundings to inspire calm, clarity and rejuvenation.
Empowerment through awareness
We promote informed, voluntary participation and self-responsibility. Healing begins when you choose to show up for yourself.
Community and connection
You are not alone. Healing is a shared experience, and here you are part of a growing family rooted in love, growth and shared purpose.
